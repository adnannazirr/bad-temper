05

Health + safety

Classes take place in a heated room. Consult your doctor before starting any new exercise program, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or are recovering from an injury.

  • Arrive hydrated and bring water and a towel.
  • Tell your instructor about any injuries before class.
  • Work at your own pace and take a break whenever you need one.
  • Stop exercising and tell your instructor if you feel dizzy, faint or unwell.
